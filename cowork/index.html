AI Automation with Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork

Overview4 min read

Lab guides for the AI Automation with Microsoft Cowork workshop. Participants move from their first Cowork task to building and publishing their own plugin.

Read the labs online: ai.ebigpictures.com/cowork

The story: your new personal assistant

In the Copilot Studio labs you build a robot, part by part. Cowork is different. You don't build anything. On day one, a personal assistant joins your team, already trained. Your job is to give it errands, then teach it your way of working, then share what you taught with the whole company.

Note

Copilot Chat answers one question at a time, like a receptionist. Cowork takes a whole errand ("prepare my week", "tidy my files") and comes back with finished work, like an assistant.

Lab 1: First day on the job. A new assistant needs a badge, a desk with real work on it and a logbook. You sign in, and Cowork works as you, with your mail, calendar, OneDrive and Teams. You fill its desk with sample emails, meetings, files and a team: that's its work context. Its task history is the logbook, and scheduled tasks are its daily routine.

Lab 2: Managing the boss's diary. The first errand is your calendar. The assistant reads your week, schedules meetings, writes a prep brief before an important one, and spots conflicts. You gave one instruction; it ran several steps.

Lab 3: Tidying the filing cabinet. Next errand: your files. It sorts and renames them, audits your OneDrive, builds an Excel tracker and posts a report in Teams. One request, three apps, one finished result.

Lab 4: Writing the procedure manual. A good assistant learns how you like things done. You write your format down once, as a procedure card: a skill. Next time you say "leadership update", it follows the card exactly.

Lab 5: The whiteboard on the wall. Some work is a routine, not an errand. The assistant builds a personal dashboard and refreshes it every hour, like a whiteboard it keeps up to date while you work.

Lab 6: The onboarding binder. You trained one assistant well; now every assistant in the company should work the same way. You package your procedure cards into a binder, a plugin, and publish it for your colleagues.

Publishing the site

In the repository, go to Settings > Pages, set Source to Deploy from a branch, pick main and / (root), and save. The site appears at https://<owner>.github.io/<repository>/ after a minute or two.

Notes

  • Copilot Cowork is AI-powered: results vary between runs and won't match the screenshots word for word.
  • Personal details (user IDs and email addresses) are blurred in all screenshots.
  • Lab credentials are provided by your lab environment and are never stored in this repository.
